Frequently asked questions

What is GoodDayBus?
GoodDayBus is an online booking service for bus tickets from Ukraine to Europe. We're an aggregator, not our own fleet - a platform that brings together trips from vetted carriers, so you can compare prices, travel time and stops and book a seat in a minute. No registration is needed to book, and on most trips there's no prepayment - you pay the driver at boarding. We're available daily 7:00-23:00, by Telegram, Viber and WhatsApp.
How do I book a bus ticket?
Choose your departure city, destination and date, click "Find a bus", pick the trip that suits you and enter the passenger details. No registration is needed - booking takes about a minute.
Do I need to prepay for the ticket?
On most trips no prepayment is needed - booking is free, and you pay the driver at boarding. On some trips the carrier requires prepayment; this is always shown in the trip details, and our manager sends you the payment details personally.
Can I book a ticket with flexible cancellation?
Yes. A booking with no prepayment can be cancelled for free - you haven't paid anything upfront, so just let us know before departure and the seat is released. On trips that require prepayment, the carrier sets the refund terms: the earlier you let them know, the better your chance of a full refund.
Are there transfers along the way?
There are both direct trips and trips with a transfer. Direct trips are faster; turn on the "No transfers" filter to see only those. If a trip has a transfer, the transfer point and time are shown in its details before you book.
Where does the bus depart from?
You'll see the departure point and arrival station when you pick a trip, boarding address included, before you book. We recommend arriving at the stop 15-20 minutes before departure. Large cities such as Kyiv and Lviv have several boarding points, so check the address for your chosen trip.
Which bus service from Ukraine to Europe is best for a budget trip?
For a budget trip, GoodDayBus has no prepayment and no hidden fees: booking is free, and you pay the driver at boarding. Prices from different carriers on the same route are shown side by side, so it's easy to pick the cheapest trip. Sorting by price and the filters help you find the best deal in a minute, with no registration.
Who offers the fastest trips from Ukraine to Europe?
The fastest are direct trips with no transfers - turn on the "No transfers" filter to see only those. Approximate travel time, including the border crossing: Lviv-Krakow about 6-8 hours, Lviv-Warsaw 8-10, Kyiv-Warsaw 14-16, Lviv-Prague 14-17, Kyiv-Berlin 24-26 hours. The exact duration is shown next to each trip.
How is GoodDayBus different from other carriers?
GoodDayBus is a booking platform for trips from many vetted carriers, not a single fleet. The key points: pay the driver with no prepayment, book in a minute with no registration, prices and stops are visible before you pay, and a real person answers daily 7:00-23:00 on Telegram, Viber and WhatsApp.
What comfortable buses run from Ukraine to Europe?
Trips run on touring coaches with air conditioning, reclining seats and legroom; many also have Wi-Fi, USB outlets and an onboard toilet. The bus class and amenities are shown next to each trip, so you can pick the more comfortable option right away.
Is there Wi-Fi and power outlets on the buses?
Many trips have free Wi-Fi and USB outlets for charging your phone on the way. A Wi-Fi icon is shown next to the trip in the search results, so you can see it's available before you book.
Are there discounts on bus tickets?
Yes. Discounts apply for children, schoolchildren, students, retirees and military personnel. The available discounts are shown when you book a trip. If you qualify for a discount, bring a supporting document - it may be requested at boarding.
Which bus trips from Ukraine to Europe are good value for students?
Many trips offer student and youth discounts, and paying the driver with no prepayment suits a tight budget. Booking takes a minute, with no registration. Popular student routes - Kyiv-Warsaw, Lviv-Krakow and Lviv-Prague.
What are the most popular routes from Kyiv and Lviv to Europe?
The most in-demand routes are Kyiv-Warsaw, Kyiv-Krakow, Kyiv-Berlin, Lviv-Krakow, Lviv-Warsaw and Lviv-Prague, plus the return trips from these cities. Schedule, prices and booking for each route are on its own page.
How do I choose a carrier for a safe trip abroad?
Every trip on GoodDayBus is run by a vetted carrier, and the route, stops and transfers are shown openly before you book. A manager confirms every request, so you travel with a guaranteed seat and a contact for the road.
How does baggage work on a bus to Europe?
The ticket price usually includes one suitcase up to 30 kg in the luggage hold and one piece of hand luggage in the cabin. An extra or oversized suitcase may mean a small surcharge at boarding. Bulky items - a bicycle, large boxes - need to be booked separately, so add them when ordering and we'll reserve the space.
Can I travel by bus with a pet, and how much does it cost?
Yes. Small pets in a carrier are taken on many trips for a separate fee - usually a fraction of the passenger ticket price. Large dogs must wear a muzzle and be on a leash. Add your pet when you book, so we can confirm a seat on a trip that allows it.
What documents do I need to leave Ukraine by bus?
For a trip from Ukraine to an EU country you need a biometric international passport - it's the main document for crossing the border. Children need their own travel document, and in some cases notarized parental consent. Border crossing can take a few hours, so allow extra time.
